Privacy Policy

Jotswift · Last updated 18 October 2026

Jotswift is a notes, planner, calendar and to-do app. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and the choices you have. In short: we keep your notebooks so they sync between your devices, we don't show ads, we don't track you, and we never sell your information.

What we collect

Your account. When you sign in with Apple, Google or an email address, we receive your email address, your name if you choose to share it, and an account ID. If you use "Hide My Email" with Apple, we only see the private relay address.

What you make. Your notebooks, pages, handwriting, typed text, tasks, events, templates you save, PDFs and documents you import, photos you add to pages or covers, and sound you record while writing. These are stored with your account so they appear on all your devices.

Purchases. When you buy Jotswift Pro or a template, the payment is handled by Apple (or Google on Android). We never see your card details. We receive a record of what you bought so it unlocks on your devices.

What we don't collect

Jotswift has no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking. We don't read or analyse your notes, and we don't use them to train any AI. The microphone is used only while you are recording, and the app only sees the photos you choose to add.

Who stores it for us

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage) stores your account and your content, encrypted in transit and at rest. See Firebase's privacy information.
  • RevenueCat keeps the record of your purchases and subscription, linked to your account ID only. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.
  • Apple and Google process payments under their own privacy policies.
  • Google Fonts: some notebook styles download their fonts from Google, which sees your device's IP address when it does.

We don't share your information with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

Reminders and notifications

Reminders for tasks and events are scheduled on your device. They are not sent through our servers.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your content for as long as you have an account. To delete everything, open Settings → Delete account in the app. This permanently erases your account and all your notebooks, files, recordings, tasks and events from our servers. Apple, Google and RevenueCat may keep purchase records as their own policies and the law require.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you can ask to see, correct, export or delete the information we hold about you, or object to how we use it. You can do most of this in the app; for anything else, contact us through the Help & support page.

Children

Jotswift is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
