Common questions

My notebooks aren't on my other device. Check that both devices are signed in to the same account (Settings shows which one) and are online. Notebooks sync within a few seconds.

I bought Pro or a template but it's locked. Open Settings → Restore purchases while signed in to the same Apple ID you bought with.

How do I cancel my subscription? On iPhone or iPad, open the Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → Jotswift. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I get a refund? Purchases are refunded by Apple: visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my account? In the app, open Settings → Delete account. Everything is erased permanently. Cancel any subscription first, as deleting your account doesn't stop Apple billing you.
